FLIPPER FROM HELL

Grayscale fighting game — browser demo

Move ←→   Jump ↑   Crouch ↓
Attack Z — forward+Z = kick, down+Z = low kick, in air = jump kick, else punch
Special / OK X or Space (special when the bar is full — jump or duck to dodge)
Block hold away from the opponent    Mute M

Click the game once so it has keyboard focus, then press X to start. You play the fighter on the left (CPU opponent on the right). Best of 3 rounds.
